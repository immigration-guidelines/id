Indonesia - immigration guidance: what was checked (2026-11-15)

Result: nothing obtained. index.tsv is empty (header only) and no download script was written,
because the only reachable official source carries no guidance to officers.

Checked:
- https://www.imigrasi.go.id/ (Directorate General of Immigration; robots.txt: allow all).
  Pages /download/peraturan, /download/produk-hukum, /download/dokumen, /download/publikasi and
  /produk-hukum/peraturan-menteri. They hold: two Ministry of Law and Human Rights regulations
  (Permenkumham 11/2024 amending the visa and stay-permit regulation, Permenkumham 19/2024 on
  passports) and one 2021 ministerial regulation, i.e. legislation (not wanted); annual
  performance report, service-satisfaction and anti-corruption survey reports, complaint-team
  decrees, a letter template, a book. The produk-hukum pages show empty tables. The rest of the
  site is applicant information (visa types, stay permits, FAQ). No circulars, technical
  instructions or SOPs for officers.
- https://jdih.imigrasi.go.id/ (the Directorate General's legal documentation network, where
  Surat Edaran and director-general regulations would be): robots.txt timed out (twice). Its
  archived robots.txt (Internet Archive captures of 2021-12 and 2022-06) reads
  "User-agent: * / Disallow: /", so the Internet Archive copies were not used either.
- https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/ (Audit Board legal database): robots.txt allows everything but
  admin paths; the first search request was answered with a Cloudflare JavaScript challenge
  (HTTP 403). Host closed.
- https://peraturan.go.id/, https://jdihn.go.id/, https://kemenimipas.go.id/ (new Ministry of
  Immigration and Corrections), https://jdih.kemenimipas.go.id/: robots.txt requests timed out.
  https://jdih.kemenkumham.go.id/: host name does not resolve.

How much real guidance: Indonesia's immigration authority publishes its laws and ministerial
regulations, but the director-general circulars (Surat Edaran), technical instructions
(Petunjuk Teknis) and SOPs by which officers decide visa, stay-permit and border cases are not
on its public website. They may be in the JDIH legal database, which forbids all crawlers in
robots.txt and was unreachable from here, so nothing was obtained.
