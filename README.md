# Immigration guidance: Indonesia

Published guidance that the authorities of Indonesia issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

**Nothing could be obtained for Indonesia.** The Directorate General of Immigration's site carries ministerial regulations, reports and applicant information but no circulars, technical instructions or procedures for officers. Its legal documentation site disallows all automated access, and the other national legal databases either answered with a bot check or did not respond. `not-obtained.tsv` lists the circulars (Surat Edaran), director-general regulations and technical instructions (Petunjuk Teknis) known to exist. Contributions are welcome.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
